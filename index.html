Is Oki ready?

It’s in early beta. We’re running it in a few Purva homes and fixing things fast, so we add homes a few at a time. Join the waitlist on WhatsApp and we’ll tell you when your spot opens.

Does my cook need a smartphone?

No. Oki calls them on a normal phone, in Hindi. They never need to read anything.

Do I approve the menu every day?

Only in the first week. After that, no reply means OK. You can change anything until your cook starts cooking.

Where do groceries come from?

Your own Instamart account and your usual sabzi and meat shops. Oki gets the order ready; you approve and pay.

What if my cook doesn’t pick up, or is on leave?

Oki tells you straight away and falls back to a simple meal you’ve already agreed on.

Can it handle our dog’s food?

Yes. Pets get their own fixed recipe, made on schedule and kept stocked.

What about privacy?

Calls are recorded only so orders come out right, then deleted. Calendar access is optional and read-only, and your cook never hears what’s on it.
